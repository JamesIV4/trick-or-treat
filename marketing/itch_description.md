# Halloween: Trick or Treat!

**A cozy Halloween night adventure. Ring every doorbell, fill your candy bucket, and save the town from a monster infestation.**

Pull on your dragon onesie, grab your glowing jack-o'-lantern bucket and head out into a moonlit neighborhood. Every street is different, every house is dressed up for Halloween, and there's candy *everywhere*: on porches, up on rooftops, along fences and floating over trampolines.

---

## Two ways to play

### 🍬 Trick or Treat
A relaxed candy hunt with no monsters and no thorns. Just you, the doorbells, the treats and the trampolines. Every lantern gate is awake from the start, so you can wander from neighborhood to neighborhood at your own pace.

### 🎃 Save the Town
Monsters have overrun the neighborhoods! Pumpkin hoppers, ghosts, spiders, bats and more pour out of their nests, and thorny vines creep across the streets and choke the front doors. Grab your fire wand, burn back the thorn nests, destroy every monster nest and free each town.

---

## Features

- **A whole night to explore.** Neighborhoods, a lit-up town high street and dark woods, streamed together into one open world. Every night is a new layout.
- **Trick-or-treat at every house.** Ring the doorbell, watch the door swing open and catch the treats. Collect a house's candy and its porch lights up.
- **Platforming made for candy.** Jump, flap and glide on dragon wings, grab ledges, vault fences and bounce off trampolines, shop awnings and car hoods to reach candy high above the street.
- **Cast fire with your wand.** Fireballs, a ground-pound fire ring and a magic spin. Defeated monsters burst like piñatas, and destroyed nests spray a fountain of candy.
- **The Pumpkin Cruiser.** Buy a magic ride-on car, summon it anywhere and cruise the night. Then repaint it in nine candy-colored schemes.
- **Hidden costumes.** Five extra costumes are hidden high above rooftops around the world: Bedsheet Ghost, Black Cat, Little Witch, Skeleton and Pumpkin. Find them and wear them from the shop.
- **Upgrades.** Spend your candy on hotter flames, quicker casting, triple fireballs, a bigger blast, a bucket lid and a candy magnet.
- **Combos.** Scoop up candy in quick succession to build your combo meter.
- **Travel gates.** Glowing lantern gates link up the places you've visited (or freed).

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard / Mouse | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD | Left stick |
| Jump (again to flap, hold to glide) | Space | A |
| Cast fire | F / Left click | Y / RB |
| Spin | E | X |
| Roll / ground pound (in the air) | Shift | B |
| Climb up a ledge / drop from a ledge | Space / Ctrl | A / B |
| Camera | Q / C | Right stick |
| Summon the car / get in and out | V | LB |
| Shop | Tab | Start |
| Switch shop tabs | Q / E | LB / RB |
| Pause | Esc | Back |
| New night | N | — |

A gamepad is recommended.
